## Résumé

Since 2016, my professional path has been shaped by a deep
commitment to podiatric excellence, leadership, and innovation. I
began by establishing and running my own medical office in Sfax,
Tunisia, where I practiced for five years, building a solid reputation
for quality care and a patient-centered approach.
In 2021, I embraced a new international challenge by joining the
prestigious Bastien Gonzalez Studios, known globally for luxury
medical pedicures. I served as Studio Manager at One&Only Reethi
Rah in the Maldives, then at One&Only Royal Mirage in Dubai. This
period was marked by close collaboration with elite spa teams,
delivering high-end treatments to celebrities, top athletes, and VIP
clientele, while mentoring junior therapists and embodying the
“Bastien Manager” philosophy.
In December 2024, I returned to Tunisia with a renewed vision: the
launch of Podomus. More than just a clinic, Podomus is a space
of excellence and education in podiatry, where I combine medical
expertise, hospitality savoir-faire, and a holistic, preventive approach
to foot health.

#### Farhat Hached hospital

#### Podiatrist

novembre 2014 - juin 2015 (8 mois)
Oncology department

As part of my commitment to specialized foot care, I conducted an in-depth
research project on the side effects of chemotherapy on the nails and skin
of the feet. Working in collaboration with an oncologist and the Head of the
Oncology Department at CHU Farhat Hached in Sousse, I developed a
detailed protocol identifying the onset and severity of dermatological side
effects based on specific chemotherapy drugs and dosage thresholds. The
goal was to prevent and manage podiatric complications in cancer patients
through tailored care — offering both medical support and comfort during
treatment. This project remains a key pillar of my practice at Podomus,
especially in my approach to vulnerable and high-risk patients.
